# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- showing empathy and kindness toward others

Unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- trolling, or personal and political attacks
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit
  permission
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues and discussions in this
repository, and wherever an individual is officially representing HOROO Innovations.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove,
edit or reject comments and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict anyone who does.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **horooinnovations@gmail.com**. Every report will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be
respected.
